Our idea

A table with a Phoenician pulse

The Phoenicians were the Mediterranean's great traders, carrying goods, ideas and flavours between ports. Mandaloun cooks in that spirit: Lebanese at the centre, generous at the table, curious at the edges.

Everything arrives to be passed around.

Our story

Mandaloun Signature

The Grand Mezze

£35 per person, minimum two

A full Lebanese table in four parts. Cold mezze first: houmous, moutabbal, tabbouleh and vine leaves. Then falafel, lamb kebbeh, rakakat cheese and moussaka from the kitchen. A mixed grill or mixed shawarma follows with rice and chips, and baklawa with mouhallabieh closes the meal.
